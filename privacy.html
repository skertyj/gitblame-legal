gitblame publisher — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 13 November 2026

gitblame publisher ("the App") is a private tool that uploads the owner's own videos to the owner's own TikTok accounts through the official TikTok API.

1. What data the App accesses

When an account authorizes the App, TikTok provides an access token and basic account info (user.info.basic). The App uses the video.upload and video.publish permissions only to upload and publish videos chosen by the account owner.

2. How data is stored

Access tokens are stored only on the owner's local computer, in a file readable only by the owner. No data is sent anywhere except TikTok's official API.

3. What the App does not do

The App does not collect, sell or share personal data of TikTok users or viewers. It does not read messages, followers or any other account data beyond what is listed above.

4. Data deletion

Access can be revoked at any time in TikTok settings (Security → Manage app permissions). Local tokens are deleted by the owner on request.

This policy may be updated. The current version is always available at this address.
